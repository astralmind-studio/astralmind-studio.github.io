Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-16 · Publisher: AstralMind Studio · Contact: astralminds.studio@gmail.com

What stays on your iPhone

Your profiles (names, birth date, time and city), charts and readings are stored on your device. We have no servers and no accounts, and we cannot see this data. Charts are calculated on the device. Reading and Ask AI text is written by Apple's on-device model where your iPhone supports Apple Intelligence.

Ads

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. Before ads, you see a consent form (Google UMP) and, on iOS, the App Tracking Transparency prompt. Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, coarse location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads, and may use it for tracking if you allow it. We never give Google your birth details, names or readings. You can change your choice in Settings → Privacy options, or in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See how Google uses data.

Purchases

If you buy a points pack, Apple processes the payment. We do not receive your payment details.

Photos

If you tap "Save to Photos" on a share card, the app asks to add a picture. It never reads your photo library.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete a profile in the app, or delete the app, to remove its data from your device.

Changes and contact

We will post changes on this page. Questions: astralminds.studio@gmail.com.
