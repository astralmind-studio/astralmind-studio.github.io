Common questions

Why is my chart different from another app? We adjust your birth time to true solar time for your birth city. You can turn this off in Settings → True Solar Time.

I don't know my birth hour. You can save a profile with an unknown time; the hour pillar is left out.

Where is my data? Birth details and readings are stored on your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes them. There is no account.

How do points work? You get 5 free points a day. A reading costs 1 point; 1 point covers 5 Ask AI questions. Bought points stay on this iPhone and cannot move to another device. Contact us if a purchase did not arrive.

Why is my reading plain text, not AI? AI-written text needs an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence. Other iPhones get a standard-text reading.

Is this a prediction? No. Readings are for reflection and are not medical, legal or financial advice.
